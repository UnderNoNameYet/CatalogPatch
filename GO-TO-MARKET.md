# CatalogPatch — first $100 launch plan

## Commercial thesis

CatalogPatch is not sold as another catalog dashboard. It is sold at the moment a merchant or migration specialist already has a failed or risky CSV import.

**Exact buyer:**

1. A merchant moving products from WooCommerce, Squarespace, or Square to Shopify now.
2. A Shopify freelancer or partner who repeatedly cleans client exports in spreadsheets.

**Promise:**

> Turn the export you already have into a reviewed Shopify worksheet, without granting store access.

**Launch offer:** $29 one-time founding licence after checkout is approved and connected.

Four sales produce $116 gross revenue.

## Why a one-time licence

Catalog migrations are episodic. A subscription creates unnecessary resistance for a merchant with one urgent project. The founding licence is easier to understand and can later become an agency tool or a per-export plan after real usage data exists.

## What buyers receive

- All current source profiles
- Unlimited browser-side conversion runs in the founding version
- Shopify worksheet export
- Finding register and mapping recipe
- Product updates released during the founding beta

Do not promise automated store import, support response times, future platforms, or successful migration outcomes until those services exist.

## 10-day execution

### Day 1 — commercial readiness

- User connects a checkout for the $29 founding licence.
- Add the final checkout URL only after a successful test purchase.
- Write a plain refund and licence statement before accepting money.
- Create a two-minute screen recording using the broken fixture and WooCommerce fixture.

### Day 2 — target list

Build a list of 40 reachable prospects:

- 20 Shopify freelancers or migration specialists with public business contact routes
- 10 active merchants publicly asking about failed CSV migration
- 10 small agencies advertising WooCommerce/Squarespace-to-Shopify migration

Do not scrape private details or send bulk spam. Use only public professional contact routes and personalize every message.

### Days 3–5 — direct validation

Send 10 focused messages per day. Lead with the workflow, not “AI” or generic software language.

**Partner message**

> I built a browser-only tool for the spreadsheet step in Shopify migrations. It detects WooCommerce/Squarespace/Square product exports, lets you approve the field map, catches duplicate SKUs and invalid prices, then generates a draft-first Shopify worksheet. No store permission is required. I’m looking for migration operators willing to test one redacted or synthetic export. If it saves you a cleanup pass, the founding licence is $29 one time. Here is the working fixture demo: [URL]

**Merchant message**

> Your post mentioned a product CSV that would not import cleanly. CatalogPatch runs locally in the browser, explains row-level blockers, and generates a Shopify worksheet after you approve the mapping. It does not ask for store access. You can try the broken fixture before using your own file: [URL]

Do not claim that a reply, recommendation, or sale exists when it does not.

### Day 6 — public launch

Publish one useful teardown rather than a promotional link dump:

- Title: “Four silent ways a store migration CSV breaks variants”
- Demonstrate comma-decimal prices, scientific-notation SKUs, WooCommerce variation parents, and image URL warnings.
- Include the free broken-file fixture and workbench at the end.
- Suitable channels: a personal build log, Indie Hackers, relevant Shopify developer communities where self-promotion is allowed, and a GitHub release.

### Days 7–8 — partner follow-up

- Follow up once with people who opened or replied.
- Offer to add one missing header alias based on a synthetic sample.
- Record objections verbatim: trust, output correctness, platform coverage, price, or lack of need.

### Days 9–10 — close or kill

Success threshold:

- 10 people complete the broken fixture
- 5 people load their own safe test file
- 2 people reach a Shopify upload preview
- 4 founding licences sold ($116)

Kill or materially reposition if nobody loads a real file after 30 personalized contacts. Do not respond by adding random features.

## Launch assets

### One-sentence pitch

Convert a WooCommerce, Squarespace, or Square product export into a reviewed Shopify worksheet without uploading the catalog or granting store access.

### Short launch post

> Store migrations often fail in the spreadsheet between platforms. CatalogPatch reads a product CSV locally, detects the source format, lets you approve the field map, flags unsafe rows, and exports a draft-first Shopify worksheet. The beta includes fictional fixtures, so you can test the workflow without sharing store data. Independent tool; Shopify preview still required. [URL]

### Demo sequence

1. Open the broken fixture.
2. Show auto-detected semicolon delimiter and generic source.
3. Approve Title, SKU, Price, Stock, Image, and Published mappings.
4. Run conversion.
5. Show duplicate SKU, invalid price, scientific-notation SKU, negative stock, and image URL findings.
6. Switch to the WooCommerce fixture.
7. Show two variations grouped under one handle with the sale price converted.
8. Download the Shopify worksheet and audit register.

## Required user actions

The product can be built and published autonomously, but revenue requires the account owner to:

1. Connect and verify a payment provider.
2. Approve the refund/licence terms.
3. Send or publish outreach from accounts they control.
4. Respond to prospective buyers and support requests.

Publishing alone is not distribution and should not be treated as a revenue strategy.
