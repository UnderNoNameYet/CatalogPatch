WHY IMPORTS BREAK

Two valid catalogs.
Two incompatible assumptions.

Platforms disagree about headers, variant grouping, price formats, image fields, booleans, and weight units. A spreadsheet may look correct while still creating duplicate products, blank prices, or broken variants.
